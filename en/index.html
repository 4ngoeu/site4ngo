Connecting civil society in Europe

4ngo is a European ecosystem that helps non-governmental organisations find information, partners and opportunities for joint projects.

Follow us How it will work

Why 4ngo

Many organisations, shared causes, rarely together

Information is scattered across dozens of websites and languages. Partners from other countries are found mostly through personal contacts. Small and local organisations stay outside European networks. 4ngo brings all of this together in one place.

The ecosystem

One ecosystem. Three doors.

  • 4ngo.eu

    Tells the story. Who we are, our mission, news and advocacy.

    You are here

  • portal4ngo.eu

    Informs. A directory of NGOs in the EU, calls and guides.

    Coming soon

  • hub4ngo.eu

    Connects. Partner search, thematic groups, training.

An organisation's journey

  1. 1

Who it is for

For the whole of civil society

  • Small and local NGOs

    Information and partners that are otherwise hard to find.

  • Networks and experienced organisations

    New members and partners for consortia.

  • Donors and institutions

    Visibility for calls and better-prepared applicants.

  • Volunteers and activists

    Causes and organisations across Europe.
